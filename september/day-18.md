# Day 18 — Ship: where is the model confident-and-wrong?

- [ ] Done

**After this you should know:** how to do error analysis — the highest-value habit in AI
engineering, and the seed of your evals.

## 📖 Read (~15 min)
[Eugene Yan — Looking at your data](https://eugeneyan.com/writing/data-flywheel/) (or
re-skim Hamel). Get: the wins hide in the cases the model got confidently wrong.

## 🛠️ Build (~20 min)
Pull the items where the model was *high-confidence* but you disagreed. Write up 3–5 of
them: what did it miss, and is it a prompt problem or a genuinely hard call?

## 🤖 Claude-craft (~10 min)
Use a **subagent** to help surface/analyze the divergences in your data.
