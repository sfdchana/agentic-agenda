## 📖 Read (~20 min)
*Designing Data-Intensive Applications* ch.1 (Reliability, Scalability, Maintainability) or the **System Design Primer** scalability section (github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer). Get: throughput vs latency, bottlenecks, reasoning about 10×.

## 🛠️ Build (~20 min) — taste-map project
Build the **taste-map view** — a 2D plot of two axes with items/designers as **photo-dots** at their scored coordinates. Static/sample data first. This is the thing you envisioned.

## Understand & defend (~5 min, journal)
Which two axes make the most *legible* map, and why? What breaks when there are 500 dots on it?
