# Podcasts

Low-energy way to keep marinating in the material — good for hard days, commutes,
chores. Curated for *my* lane (AI engineering + clean data for AI), not a generic
list.

> The links open Spotify straight to the show (search deep-links — tap and it's
> the top result).

## Start here (the two to actually begin with)

- **Latent Space** — swyx & Alessio Fanelli. The definitive "AI engineer"
  podcast: agents, evals, RAG, inference, shipping to production. Closest match to
  my whole plan. → [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Latent%20Space%20AI%20Engineer%20Podcast)
- **Practical AI** — Chris Benson & Daniel Whitenack (Changelog). The application
  layer: MLOps, evaluation harnesses, the unglamorous parts of shipping AI.
  → [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Practical%20AI%20Changelog%20podcast)

## AI engineering — agents & evals (Months 1, 3, 4)

- **The Cognitive Revolution** — Nathan Labenz. Deep on real model *evaluations*
  and capabilities, builder's POV — pairs with the Month 4 evals work.
  → [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Cognitive%20Revolution%20Nathan%20Labenz)
- **TWIML AI Podcast** — Sam Charrington. Deepest archive in the field; focused
  technical interviews on ML research, MLOps, operationalizing AI.
  → [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/TWIML%20AI%20Podcast)

## Data engineering (Month 2 — the clean-data lane)

- **Data Engineering Podcast** — Tobias Macey. The tools, techniques, and hard
  parts of data engineering. Directly the Month 2 muscle.
  → [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Data%20Engineering%20Podcast%20Tobias%20Macey)
- **The Data Engineering Show** — practitioners on their real day-to-day pipeline
  problems (has covered RAG + agent automation).
  → [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/The%20Data%20Engineering%20Show)

## Practical LLM + RAG (Months 3 & 5)

- **DataTalks.Club Podcast** — practical LLM engineering and RAG: prompting,
  evaluation, real-world workflows.
  → [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/DataTalks.Club%20podcast)

## For depth / big picture (any time, esp. hard days)

- **Dwarkesh Podcast** — long, deep conversations with frontier researchers. More
  "where is this going" than how-to — good for perspective and motivation.
  → [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/search/Dwarkesh%20Podcast)

---

**How to use these:** not study — no notes required. Passive immersion. Hearing
practitioners use the vocabulary (evals, RAG, tool-calling, context windows)
casually makes it feel normal faster. If a line sparks something, drop it in
`../journal/ideas.md`.
