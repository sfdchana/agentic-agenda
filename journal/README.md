# Journal

A place to offload my brain as I go so nothing gets lost. End **every session**
with a line or two here (the last 2 minutes). Three files:

- `notes.md` — running log: what I did, what clicked, small realizations.
- `questions.md` — open questions I couldn't answer in the moment (revisit later).
- `ideas.md` — project ideas, features, "what if" — fuel for later months.

Convention: prefix an a-ha line with `!`. Next session, turn `!` lines into
flashcards (rewriting them in my own words is the actual learning).
