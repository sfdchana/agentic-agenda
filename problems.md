# Problems worth solving — resale + fashion-tech

A living dump of **real pain points** in resale / archival / fashion, captured as
they hit me. My edge isn't "I can wire an LLM to an API" — everyone can. It's that
I *am* the resale consumer and I know where it actually hurts. Projects aimed at
real pain read as "she gets the user," which is the rarest signal a fashion-tech
company can find in an engineer.

**How to use this:** throw anything in, messy is fine. Note where it came from
(my own shopping / a podcast / browsing Depop). Star ⭐ the ones that make me go
*"I would genuinely use that."* When we scope a build (~Month 2 on), we pull from
here — not from "what's easy to demo."

**Is a problem worth building for? Quick lens:**
- Who feels it, and how often? (daily friction > rare annoyance)
- Is it a real *job to be done*, or just a cool idea?
- Does my consumer insight give an edge a generic ML engineer wouldn't have?
- Is there data to work with (my archive, resale APIs, images)?

## Seeds — categories + starter pain points (react to these, add your own)

### Discovery / search — "finding the good piece in the sea of junk"
- Resale search is keyword + filter only; I can't search by *taste* ("something
  structured but a little off", "downtown, not preppy"). ⭐ (this is taste-search)
- The good stuff is buried under thousands of mediocre listings; no "sort by taste."
- I follow 6 platforms; there's no way to search them all at once.

### Fit & sizing — "will it actually fit me?"
- Vintage/archival sizing is nothing like modern sizing; a 60s "medium" is a mystery.
- Every brand and era sizes differently; measurements are listed inconsistently or not at all.
- No try-on; I'm guessing from a flat photo and a seller's vague note.

### Authentication & trust — "is it real, is it as described?"
- Fakes everywhere; hard to tell from photos.
- Condition is subjective — "good condition" hides flaws until it arrives.
- Seller photos vs reality gap.

### Pricing — "is this a fair price / what will it hold?"
- Pricing is opaque; no sense of what a piece *actually* goes for.
- No price history; is $180 a deal or a rip-off?
- As a seller: what do I list this at? What actually sells?

### Longevity / taste — "fad or keeper?"
- I can't tell if a piece is a passing microtrend or something that lasts. ⭐ (survives_trend)
- Cost-per-wear is a guess made after the fact.

### Selling / listing side — the seller's pain
- Listing is tedious: photograph, measure, describe, price, cross-post to 5 apps.
- Writing a good description that surfaces in search is hard.
- Knowing what's worth listing at all.

### Post-purchase — "did I make a good decision?"
- No view of my wardrobe's actual cost-per-wear or resale value over time.
- What pairs with what I already own? (composition)
